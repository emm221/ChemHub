Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

ChemHub is a student-run academic resource initiative for Chemistry students at Federal University of Lafia. It is created and managed by Emmanuel Musa. It is an independent student-led initiative. This policy explains what information the site uses and why.

1. What we collect

When you create an account: your full name, email address, academic level, department (Chemistry), and an optional matriculation number. Your password is handled by our authentication service and is not stored in readable form. When you use the site: the resources you save as bookmarks, and, for administrators, a record of management actions such as uploading or deleting a resource.

2. Why we use it

To create and secure your account, let you sign in, show your profile, let you save and download study materials, and run and protect the platform. We do not sell your information and we do not show advertising.

3. Who can see it

Your profile, including your matriculation number, is visible only to you and to ChemHub administrators. Saved resources are visible only to you. Other students cannot see your details.

4. Services that process data

ChemHub uses Supabase to provide sign-in, the database, and file storage. The website pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Fonts load from Google Fonts and a code library loads from jsDelivr, so those providers can see your IP address and device information when a page loads. Each provider has its own privacy policy.

5. Browser storage

To keep you signed in, the site stores a session token in your browser’s local storage. It is removed when you sign out or clear your browser data. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Downloads

Study materials are kept in private storage. Download links are created only for signed-in students and expire after a short time.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account information while your account is active. You can edit your name, level, and matriculation number on your profile page. To have your account and data deleted, send a request through the Contact form on the homepage.

8. Your rights

We aim to handle personal data in line with applicable Nigerian data protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. You may ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you using the Contact form.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the platform grows. The date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact

Use the Contact form on the homepage.
